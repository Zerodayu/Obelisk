OBELISK — CQI Institutional Report Development Brief
Objective
Develop a CQI Institutional Reporting Module that consolidates educational assessment data and transforms it into meaningful statistical information, institutional findings, and evidence-based improvement plans.
The report is an end-of-cycle deliverable intended to support academic administrators, institutional evaluation, and applicable regulatory or accreditation documentation.
Three fundamental questions
What is happening to our students' alignment with the intended educational outcomes?
What is the actual situation of our students and academic performance?
How can the institution address the identified problems and improve outcomes?

Technical requirements
Statistics must be calculated from actual database records.
All percentages must have traceable numerators and denominators.
Performance thresholds must follow the 70% floor and the F04 Target-Setting Matrix targets, on the rounded composite.
Missing and incomplete records must be identified.
Historical comparisons must use comparable assessment data.
AI must never generate or fabricate statistical values.
AI recommendations must be traceable to actual findings and Saved as pending_review, generated by VPAA and system_admin (or whoever you decide), never auto-approved..
Reports must protect student privacy.(Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) compliance: aggregates only, no names or IDs sent to the LLM, small-group suppression)
The report must support export into a presentable institutional document, preferably PDF.
Acceptance criterion
The final CQI report must be understandable and meaningful when presented independently to academic administrators and institutional evaluators.
A reader must be able to understand:
What was assessed.
What the statistical results indicate.
Whether intended educational outcomes were achieved.
What institutional problems were identified.
What interventions were implemented or proposed.
Whether previous interventions produced measurable improvements.
What actions should be taken in the next assessment cycle.
Core development principle: OBELISK must not merely identify students at risk. It must transform educational data into institutional intelligence that supports evidence-based decision-making and continuous quality improvement.
