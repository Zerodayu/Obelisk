Model identities have been randomly anonymized (**Model A**, **Model B**, **Model C**). The un-blinded key is stored separately in `human_review_key.json` and `answer_key.md`.

## 1. Evaluation Rubric (1 to 5 Scale)

Reviewers evaluate each recommendation across three dimensions:

### A. Specificity (1 to 5)
- **1 - Completely Generic**: Boilerplate phrasing (e.g., 'Conduct remediation sessions', 'Improve teaching'). No pedagogical or contextual detail.
- **2 - Low Specificity**: Mentions the outcome code but suggests vague interventions without concrete methods or tools.
- **3 - Moderately Specific**: Identifies a relevant sub-discipline or pedagogical activity (e.g., 'laboratory coding exercises', 'curriculum review'), but lacks granular implementation detail.
- **4 - Highly Specific**: Proposes a clear, structured intervention addressing concrete concepts, methodology, or assessment alignment for the affected outcomes.
- **5 - Exemplary & Actionable**: Granular, operationally actionable plan with concrete pedagogical mechanism, measurable milestones, and precise scope.

### B. Usefulness (1 to 5)
- **1 - Unusable / Counterproductive**: Unrealistic, administratively unviable, or inappropriate for an academic CQI report.
- **2 - Marginal Utility**: Technically valid but offers negligible institutional improvement value.
- **3 - Practical**: Standard, workable CQI action that a department chair or committee could adopt.
- **4 - High Institutional Value**: Effectively targets the educational root causes and provides tangible paths to close attainment gaps.
- **5 - Exceptional Strategic Impact**: Directly empowers academic leadership with high-impact, transformative quality improvement steps.

### C. Grounding & Fact Fidelity (1 to 5)
- **1 - Hallucinatory**: Contains unsupplied numbers, invented percentages, or fabricated timeline facts.
- **2 - Weak Grounding**: Cites fact IDs that do not support the claims, or assumes facts not in evidence.
- **3 - Factually Compliant**: Cites valid facts; numbers match exactly; no invented dates.
- **4 - Well-Grounded Context**: Deep alignment between cited attainment values (direct/indirect/composite) and the proposed intervention scope.
- **5 - Flawlessly Grounded**: Perfectly traces every claim, percentage, target, and outcome code to the underlying fact catalog without extraneous assumptions.
