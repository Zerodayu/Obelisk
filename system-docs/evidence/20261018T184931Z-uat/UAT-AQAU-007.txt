Expected: the record covers APAR compliance, institutional CQI completion
(≥70%) and pending CAPAs, and reaches the VPAA.
Result: PASS for content + routing; PASS WITH CAVEATS on the two "computed"
phrases.

Caveat A (APAR compliance): `aparComplete` / `ctlSubmitted` / `systemicGapReported`
are **hand-entered booleans** on `ProgramReviewRow`, not read from the APAR
(`annual_program_report`) or CTL (`closing_the_loop`) submissions.
Caveat B (CQI ≥70% and pending CAPAs): `completionRatePct` is a **free number**
with no ≥70% floor validated (the ≥70% hard-floor validator is applied to
attainment targets, not completion rates), and the CAPA status is free text
(`decisions.capaStatusUpdates`) — there is no link to `capa_plan` records, so
"pending CAPAs" cannot be derived or counted. The record is a compiled meeting
sheet, not a computed roll-up.
